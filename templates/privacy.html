Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application. We believe in keeping things simple: your financial data is yours, and we only handle it as much as needed to make the service work.

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the basic information needed to identify and protect your account — namely your email address and a hashed version of your password. When you use the service, we store the expense records you choose to add, including the amount, category, date, and any description you provide. We also collect limited technical information such as the device and browser you use, login timestamps, and basic error diagnostics, so we can keep the service stable and secure.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, card numbers, or government identifiers. Spendly is a manual expense tracker — the only financial data we ever see is what you type in yourself.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for a small, well-defined set of purposes: to create and maintain your account, to store and display the expense records you enter, to generate the summaries and category breakdowns you see in the app, to detect and prevent abuse or security incidents, and to communicate with you about important service changes.

We do not sell your data. We do not use the content of your expense records for advertising, profiling, or any form of marketing. We do not share your data with third parties except as described in section 4, or as required to comply with a valid legal request.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers operated by reputable infrastructure providers. We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information in transit and at rest, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords. Access to production data is restricted to a small number of authorized individuals who need it to operate and support the service.

While no system is perfectly secure, we work continuously to reduce risk. If we ever become aware of a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities in line with applicable law. You can delete your account and all associated expense data at any time from your account settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third-party services to operate. These include a hosting provider that stores our database, an email service used to send account-related messages such as sign-up confirmations and password resets, and a basic error-monitoring tool that helps us diagnose crashes. Each of these providers receives only the minimum information needed to perform its function and is bound by its own privacy and security obligations.

We do not integrate advertising networks, social-media trackers, or analytics tools that follow you across other sites. If we introduce a new third-party integration in the future, we will update this policy to reflect it.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and update your account information at any time. You can export your expense data and you can permanently delete your account from the account settings. If you have questions about your data or want to exercise rights afforded to you by applicable law (such as access, correction, or deletion), you can reach us through the contact options listed in section 6.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or about how your data is handled, please reach out through the support options available in the app. We read every message and will do our best to respond promptly.
